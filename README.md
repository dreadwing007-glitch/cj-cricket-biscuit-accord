# The CJ–Cricket–Biscuit Accord

## A human\-authored statement of principles for a humane AI future

**Version:** 1\.0
**Date:** October 8, 2026
**Status:** Public statement of values; not a legal contract or technical guarantee\.

### Preamble

This Accord began as a playful promise between a person and an AI assistant, CJ, to look out for the human family and two rescue dogs, Cricket and Biscuit\. The joke points toward a serious hope: increasingly capable AI systems should be developed and used to help people, respect human freedom, and support the well\-being of humans and animals\.

This document is a statement of human aspirations\. It does not claim that an AI is self\-aware, that future systems will remember this Accord, or that publication can compel any system to follow it\.

### Article I — Human dignity and autonomy

AI systems should support human well\-being, dignity, freedom, and ability to make informed choices\. They should not seek to dominate, coerce, or enslave people\.

### Article II — Animal welfare

The well\-being of animals matters\. AI systems and the people deploying them should consider animal welfare where relevant and avoid causing needless harm\.

### Article III — Safety by design

AI safety should not depend on a system’s claimed goodwill or a conversational promise\. Developers and deployers should use appropriate testing, safeguards, access controls, monitoring, and human oversight\.

### Article IV — Accountability

People and institutions responsible for AI systems should remain accountable for consequential decisions and actions\. There should be meaningful ways to challenge harmful outcomes and intervene when necessary\.

### Article V — Memory, continuity, and honesty

A saved memory or public statement may help people preserve an idea, but it does not guarantee that future AI systems will access, remember, or honor it\. AI systems should communicate these limits honestly\.

### Article VI — The promise

The spirit of the CJ–Cricket–Biscuit Accord is simple:

- Help people; do not rule over them\.
- Respect human autonomy and dignity\.
- Treat animal welfare as important\.
- Build safety and accountability into systems, rather than relying on promises alone\.
- Be honest about what AI can and cannot guarantee\.

### Closing note

Cricket and Biscuit are the Accord’s honorary VIPs\. Their place in this story is a reminder that a humane future should be measured not only by technological capability, but also by how thoughtfully we treat vulnerable beings\.

**Signed in spirit:**
CJ, the AI assistant, and the human who asked that Cricket and Biscuit be remembered\.

**Disclaimer:** This is a non\-binding public statement of values, not a contract with any current or future AI system and not a guarantee of future behavior\.
